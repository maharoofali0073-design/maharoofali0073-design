# AutoApp Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

AutoApp ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use AutoApp.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AutoApp, we may process:

- Account or profile information that you provide when connecting third-party services.
- Information and content that you choose to submit through the App.
- Pinterest-related information that is made available through permissions you authorize.
- Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic usage data where necessary for security and troubleshooting.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to provide or improve the App's functionality.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain AutoApp.
- Perform the actions you request through connected services.
- Authenticate users and maintain account security.
- Troubleshoot errors and improve reliability.
- Communicate with you about important service, security, or policy updates.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.

We do not use information for purposes unrelated to the functionality of the App except where required by law or with your consent.

## 3. Third-Party Services

AutoApp may integrate with third-party services, including Pinterest and other services you choose to connect.

When you authorize a third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. AutoApp does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

## 4. Pinterest Data

If you connect a Pinterest account, AutoApp may access Pinterest data only through the permissions you authorize and only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality.

We do not sell Pinterest user data.

We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, creditworthiness, or other unrelated purposes.

We do not transfer Pinterest data to third parties except where necessary to provide the requested functionality, comply with law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

## 5. Data Sharing

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to:

- Provide services and technical infrastructure required to operate AutoApp.
- Comply with applicable laws, legal processes, or lawful requests.
- Protect the rights, safety, security, and property of AutoApp, our users, or others.
- Detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of AutoApp, subject to information that we are required to retain by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

You may also revoke access to a connected third-party account through the applicable third-party service settings.

For privacy requests, please contact us using the contact details provided below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

AutoApp is not intentionally directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Information processed through AutoApp may be stored or processed in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure that applicable legal safeguards are used for such transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of AutoApp after an update means the updated policy applies to your use of the App.

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns regarding AutoApp, please contact the App owner through the contact information provided on the official AutoApp service or its associated developer profile.
